8. Handling JavaScript Alert During Click
- Clicking the Copy option triggers a JavaScript alert.
- The alert blocks further browser actions.
- Playwright may wait for click() to complete while the alert is open. - complete doesn't mean that just 
click its completed - playwright also do validation post click - so those post validation will blocked due to js alert.
- Solution: Handle the dialog and click simultaneously using Promise.all().
await Promise.all([
    copyOption.click(),
    dialogPromise
]);
